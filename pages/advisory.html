FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who do you typically work with?

Founders and leadership teams at companies between roughly $500K and $20M in revenue — or earlier-stage teams preparing to raise. If your finances feel one step behind your growth, we're very likely a fit.

What happens on the free consultation?

A 30-minute video call. You bring your biggest financial challenge; we give you a candid read on it and at least one actionable recommendation. No pitch, no obligation — and you'll know exactly what working together would look like and cost.

How do engagements and pricing work?

Two models: fixed-scope projects (fundraising prep, model builds, diagnostics) and monthly fractional-CFO retainers. Pricing is confirmed in writing after your diagnostic — no surprise invoices, and no long lock-in contracts.

Do you work remotely?

Fully remote, across time zones. We work inside the tools you already use — Xero, QuickBooks, Excel or Google Sheets — so there's nothing new for your team to learn.
