Why Brightside

We schedule like it matters. Because it does.

Most dental trouble starts as small stuff nobody had time to explain. So we flipped the usual rush: longer visits, cameras that show you what we see, and nothing happening in your mouth until you’ve approved the plan and the price.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    Your first visit is a 50-minute conversation — history, worries, goals. No chair time unless you want it.

  2. 02

    Show, then tell

    An intraoral camera puts what we see on-screen, and you leave with printed options and prices. No surprises, ever.

  3. 03

    Treat gently

    Numbing that actually numbs, sedation for the white-knucklers, and breaks whenever you raise a hand.

Nervous? Your first visit can be a lounge chat — no chair, no pressure, no drill soundtrack.

02 · Typography

Bricolage Grotesque

Display · weights 600–800 · tracking-tight

Big on care, light on dread.

  • H1 clamp(42 → 70px) · H2 clamp(30 → 36px) · H3 18–20px extrabold
  • No italics in this family — emphasis uses the butter “highlight mark”
Ag

Figtree

Body & UI · 400 / 500 / 600 / 700

Body copy runs 15–18px at 1.6 line height. Labels and chips are bold with wide uppercase tracking; nothing on the page is set lighter than 400.

  • Line length capped near 65 characters
  • Underlines are 2px and decorative-coloured (coral), never default

03 · Iconography

Lucide set, 2px stroke (chunkier than default to match the borders). 16–20px inline, 24px+ for feature moments. Coral marks actions; plum marks labels; the only filled icon is the amber review star.

04 · Imagery

  • Real rooms, real team, warm daylight — candid beats posed, always.
  • Flat geometric accents (sparkles, dots, butter highlights) over photos and dark panels.
  • Photos get 2px plum borders + hard shadows so they sit “on” the page, not in it.
  • Never: blue clinical gradients, glossy 3-D teeth, or laughing-with-props stock smiles.
