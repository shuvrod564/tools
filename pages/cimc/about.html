Our Mission & Vision

Why the Institute exists

Mission

To advance communication competencies, media development, institutional effectiveness and international cooperation for public benefit — connecting people and institutions so they can inform, learn and act.

  • Foster international cooperation with public benefit at the centre

Institutions and communities everywhere communicate clearly, learn continuously and cooperate across borders — with Canadian values of accessibility, inclusion and bilingualism informing how we work.

Institutional Profile

A public-benefit mandate, plainly stated

The Canadian Institute for Media and Communication was established in Canada as a nonprofit institution with a clear public-benefit mandate: to advance communication competencies, media development, institutional effectiveness and international cooperation.

Its programmes, resources and partnerships exist to serve those institutional purposes. Where CIMC undertakes paid training, institutional services or contractual projects, it does so only where consistent with that mandate. Being nonprofit does not mean being non-commercial in every activity — it means that activities and resources must serve institutional purposes.

CIMC describes its role through public-benefit outcomes rather than commercial claims about market dominance, technology ownership or global scale.

  • Public-benefit mandate
  • Education and capacity building
  • Applied research & knowledge translation

CIMC is not

  • A for-profit company
  • A commercial business mandate
  • Client services and consulting
  • Commercial project execution
  • Business development and contracts
  • Commercial partnerships and investment activity

Technology collaborators — including Renaiscent — are acknowledged as collaborators where relevant, and are never presented as owners, operators or governing partners of CIMC.

Professional Conduct

The standards we hold

Code of Professional Conduct — commitments

  • Integrity first — act honestly and transparently in every programme, project and partnership.
  • Evidence-based communication — no unsupported figures or inflated claims; documented outcomes only.
  • Respect & inclusion — treat people and communities with dignity; design for accessibility.
  • Confidentiality & privacy — protect partner information and member data.
  • Accessibility — target WCAG 2.2 AA across what we build and deliver.
The Code is part of membership: it is accepted within the membership process on cimc.education, and is expected across all CIMC programmes, projects and partnerships.
