02 — About CIMC

A Canadian institution with an international outlook

CIMC is a Canadian, internationally engaged nonprofit institution working at the intersection of media, communication, education, research, technology and institutional capacity building.

We describe our role through public-benefit outcomes — not market claims. The Institute exists to advance communication competencies, strengthen institutions and connect a professional community across borders.

07 — Membership & Professional Learning

A professional community — not just a website

CIMC operates a real membership and learning ecosystem. Joining connects you to professional development, knowledge exchange and an operational platform — not a brochure.

  • Group 1 courses — CIMC-authored courses, included with membership.
  • Group 2 courses — partner-provided courses at applicable member discounts.
  • Professional networking, knowledge exchange and participation opportunities.
  • A clear route to the existing learning platform at cimc.education.

We describe offerings precisely: professional development, continuing professional education and certificate programmes. CIMC is not a degree-awarding university, and “accredited” language is used only where accreditation can be demonstrated.
