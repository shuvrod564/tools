Santosh Shail

Founder · Educational Media
🎓 Educational Media Consultant

Santosh Shail has over 35 years' international experience in educational content creation for distance education, providing ICT expertise across on-line Learning Management Systems, synchronous and asynchronous learning. He has comprehensive experience producing broadcast media — educational television, documentaries, PSAs, news, telefilms and corporate publicity — and has worked on international development projects for UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO, World Bank and Global Affairs Canada. He served as Educational Media Consultant for the Indira Gandhi National Open University, India, and the Commonwealth of Learning, Canada. Santosh received the 2007 'International Media Achievement Award' in India for his accomplishments in global education projects and capacity building in developing communities across Africa and Asia.

Sam Lanfranco's photoexisting photo replaces this tile

Sam Lanfranco

Development Economics
🏛 Prof. Emeritus, York University

Sam Lanfranco, Professor Emeritus & Senior Scholar at York University, Toronto, is a development economist with a PhD in Economics from the University of California at Berkeley. He has taught for over 50 years, with breaks to work for the UN, the World Bank, and various national and non-governmental development organizations. His work focuses on three areas: (1) the rights and duties of digital citizenship in the Internet ecosystem and the integrity of digital governance, business, and social media practices; (2) Director with the Society for the Advancement of Science in Africa (SASA); and (3) Advisor to the Mumbai-based Ambedkar Centre for Justice and Peace (ACJP). Sam's work is guided by a quest for greater social justice and the belief that "We can get there from here" by engaging all stakeholders and asking the right questions about context, expertise, knowledge, and technology, as much as by having access to resources.

Lalith Gunaratne

Leadership · Mindfulness
🧭 Cross-Cultural Leadership Coach

Lalith Gunaratne is a cross-cultural leadership coach, business strategist, and mindfulness advocate with over 15 years of experience working across Asia, Africa, and North America. He co-founded Sage Training in Sri Lanka and Sage Ontario in Canada, promoting mindful, values-based leadership. Lalith blends mindfulness, cultural sensitivity, and ethical grounding to help leaders foster inclusive, resilient organizations. His clients span private, public, and non-profit sectors globally. A Certified Coach in Canada and British Council-trained facilitator, Lalith holds credentials in engineering, marketing, and business responsibility, and is a published thought leader on sustainability, leadership, and well-being. He has led numerous training programs, specializing in strategic communications, emotional intelligence, and conflict resolution — bridging cultural differences while aligning leadership with purpose and impact.
