How students get access

1

Contact CIMC

Schools, programs and students connect with the Canadian Institute of Mass Communication to request Online On-Demand access.

2

Receive your access

Students receive portal credentials, or a customized mobile device pre-configured with the e-content for remote regions.

3

Learn at your pace

Stream any of 1,626 syllabus-aligned lessons on demand — self-paced, anytime learning for grades 9–12.
