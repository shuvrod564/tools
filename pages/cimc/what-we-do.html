What We Do

Five concise programme pillars — each anchored in specific examples rather than vague capability claims, and each connected to evidence across this site.

Media & Strategic Communication Education & Capacity Building Digital Learning & Technology Knowledge Translation Institutional Development & Cooperation

How we describe our work

  • Specific examples — never vague capability claims
  • Honest status labels — active, developing, or proposed
  • Linked to evidence — projects, platform and partners

“Meeting Thresholds. Setting Benchmarks.”

01

Media & Strategic Communication

We help institutions inform, engage and serve their publics — strengthening media capacity and strategic communication practice for public benefit.

Active

Where to see it

Ethiopia project Partnerships

In practice — specific examples

  • Educational television programming — production experience from our Ethiopia engagement, with partner broadcasters.
  • Production mentorship & hands-on media training — camera, audio and field-production skills for practitioners.
  • Strategic communication support — helping institutions communicate programmes, policies and results clearly.
  • Media development initiatives — strengthening media capacity with partner organizations.
02

Education & Capacity Building

We design and deliver training and institutional programmes that build durable communication competencies in organizations and communities.

Active

Where to see it

Uganda project Learning Portal

In practice — specific examples

  • Blended learning programmes — combining in-person and online delivery for working professionals.
  • Institutional education & capacity development — initiatives delivered with Ugandan partner institutions.
  • Facilitator & trainer development — building the people who teach, so capacity compounds.
  • Curriculum-aligned programme design — developed with partner institutions to fit their context.
03

Digital Learning & Technology Adoption

We design digital and blended learning, and support responsible, inclusive adoption of technology in education.

Active — via cimc.education

Where to see it

cimc.education Membership
Proposed, not established: the AI-enabled sign-language initiative sits under Research & Innovation as a proposed opportunity — not an existing product.

In practice — specific examples

  • Course delivery on our platform — CIMC-authored Group 1 courses included with membership, on cimc.education.
  • Digital & blended course design — for continuing professional education and certificate programmes.
  • Responsible technology adoption — inclusive by design; no "AI-first transformation at any cost."
  • Digital skills capacity building — for institutions adopting new learning tools and workflows.
04

Knowledge Translation

We make research and expertise accessible, usable and actionable for practitioners and decision-makers.

Developing

Where to see it

Research & Innovation

In practice — specific examples

  • Publications & insights — drawing on programme and research activity, published once approved.
  • Communication & dissemination for consortia — our defined role in research partnerships.
  • Plain-language, accessible outputs — meeting accessibility standards for every audience.
  • Knowledge exchange — structured sharing across CIMC's professional community.
05

Institutional Development & International Cooperation

We work through partnerships to strengthen institutions and cooperate across borders — consistent with SDG target 17.8 on technology and capacity-building cooperation.

Active Opportunities open

Where to see it

Partner with CIMC SDG 17

In practice — specific examples

  • Consortium coordination — organizing partners, timelines and communication for collaborative work.
  • Stakeholder engagement — connecting universities, training institutions, nonprofits and technical partners.
  • International programme cooperation — cross-border delivery with verified institutional partners.
  • Collaboration opportunities — proposed work, clearly labelled, seeking institutional partners.

How we work

Four principles behind every programme

Evidence over claims

Documented outcomes, verified against records — no artificial impact counters.

Partnership-first

Roles stated precisely, statuses labelled honestly — formal, current, or open.

Precise, restrained language

Confident and evidence-based — with the institutional voice we publish on About.

Inclusive & accessible

WCAG 2.2 AA as our target — bilingual architecture from the outset.

Bring us a programme, a partnership, or a learning need.
