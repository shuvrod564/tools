Why Elmwood

Slow dentistry, done properly.

Most dental problems start as small ones nobody had time to explain. So we flipped the usual rush: longer first visits, plain language, and a plan you approve before anything happens in your mouth.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    Your first visit is a 50-minute conversation — history, worries, goals. No chair time unless you want it.

  2. 02

    Show, then tell

    An intraoral camera puts what we see on a screen, and you leave with printed options and prices — not a surprise.

  3. 03

    Treat gently

    Numbing that actually numbs, sedation for the white-knucklers, and breaks whenever you raise a hand.

Nervous patients are our speciality — the first visit can be a chat in the lounge, no chair required.

Ag

Instrument Sans

Body & UI · 400 / 500 / 600

Body copy runs 15–18px at 1.6–1.65 line height. UI labels are 14px semibold; eyebrows are 11px caps with wide tracking.

  • Never underline body copy — underlines are reserved for links
  • Line length capped near 65 characters

03 · Iconography

Lucide set, 1.5px stroke. 16–20px inline in UI, 24px+ for feature moments. Clay when accenting an action, pine when labelling. The only filled icon is the review star.

04 · Imagery

  • Use your real rooms, team and instruments — warm daylight, candid over posed.
  • People mid-moment (laughing with the hygienist), never gurning stock smiles.
  • Illustration = single-weight continuous line, sage on bone. One motif per view.
  • Never: blue clinical gradients, glossy 3-D teeth, or models who clearly don’t floss.
