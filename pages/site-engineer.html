SECTION 02 — METHOD

Supervision is a sequence,
not a slogan.

  1. Mobilise

    We walk the site, read the drawings, and agree the standard before work starts.

  2. Set the line

    Baseline programme, QA checklists and inspection hold points — fixed, written, shared.

  3. Inspect & report

    Daily presence, hold-point checks, photo-documented findings and clear actions.

  4. Sign off

    Snags closed, records handed over, your build signed off to spec.
