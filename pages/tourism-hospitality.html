The process

Four steps.
No fee to enter.

Begin an entry →
01
Confirm eligibility
Verify your candidate status — student, educator, or accredited centre — through your CTH portal credentials. Eligibility is checked against enrolment records.
02
Prepare submission
Each category requires a written case (max 1,200 words), two references, and a portfolio of supporting material. Templates are provided in the portal.
03
Submit by 30 June
Upload through the portal. You'll receive confirmation and a panel reference number within 72 hours. Edits are permitted until the deadline.
04
Attend ceremony
Shortlisted candidates are notified by 1 October. The ceremony takes place 27 November at The Brewery, London. Black tie, brief speeches.

No. Entries are free for all accredited CTH centres, their enrolled students, and registered educators. The awards are funded by the Confederation and industry sponsors; we have never charged a submission fee and have no plans to.

Any student currently enrolled at a CTH-accredited centre, any educator employed by one, and the centres themselves. The Outstanding Student and Future Leader categories require the candidate to be in their final year of study.

Two blind rounds. Round one is a longlist review by a panel of 12 practitioners, scoring against four published criteria. Round two is a shortlist review by the full panel of 38. No judge reviews an entry from their own institution.

Centres may submit entries across multiple categories, but no individual may be entered in more than two. We discourage scattergun submissions — the written case for each entry should be specific to its category, not adapted from another.

Longlist notifications are sent by 15 July. Shortlisted candidates are contacted personally by 1 October. If you have not heard by these dates, your entry has not progressed — written feedback is available on request after the ceremony.
