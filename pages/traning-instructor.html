Section III · Approach

The way
I work.

Three principles I keep returning to. They're unspectacular. That's the point.

i.

Train the movement, not the mirror.

We start every program by making the basics look right — hinge, squat, push, pull, carry. Aesthetics follow mechanics. Trying to reverse the order is how people get hurt, or bored, or both.

ii.

Program for the next decade.

I'd rather you add five pounds to your deadlift this year and still be training at seventy than add fifty and quit by forty-five. Every block is built with that horizon in mind. Some weeks we go backwards on purpose.

iii.

The boring fundamentals, done well.

No surface-balancing, no slosh pipes, no "functional" circuits that look better on Instagram than they feel in your back. You'll do fewer exercises than you expect, with more attention than you've ever had on them.
