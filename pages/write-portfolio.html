I'm Marta. I write and edit long-form essays from a flat in Cedofeita, Porto. I work with a small number of writers and publishers each year — mostly on books, long magazine pieces, and the occasional translation from Portuguese to English, or back.

Before going independent in 2021, I was a senior editor at Granta Portugal for six years. I still take on roughly four editing projects a year, and I write one or two essays of my own.

§ How I work

Three things I keep
coming back to.

i.

Read it aloud, always.

If a sentence doesn't sound right in the mouth, it won't read right on the page. I read every edit aloud at least twice before it goes back to the writer.

ii.

Edit with a pencil first.

On paper, not on screen. The screen makes everything feel finished. The pencil reminds you it isn't — and slows you down enough to notice what's actually there.

iii.

Leave more on the floor.

The best edit is usually the one where you take the last paragraph away. Writers hate this, at first. They thank me later — sometimes months later, but they thank me.

§ Contact

Get in touch.

Email is the best way to reach me. I read everything, usually reply within a day or two. I'm not on social media — if you want to see what I'm working on, the journal is where it appears.

For editing enquiries, please include a rough word count, a deadline (if you have one), and a sample of the piece. I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right editor for it.
